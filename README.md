# Haskurit – Business Website for Tool & Equipment Rental
🌐 Live Website: https://www.haskurit.co.il/

## Overview
Haskurit is a business website developed for a real client in the field of construction tools and equipment rental.  
The site combines a clear business presentation with a dynamic product catalog and a dedicated management area.

The system allows the business owner to manage products and content independently, while providing customers with direct and accessible contact options.

The project was developed end-to-end, including requirement analysis, system design, development, and deployment.

## My Role
Full responsibility for the project – working directly with the client, defining requirements, developing the system, and deploying it to production.
